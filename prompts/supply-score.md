# Supply Score

Every opportunity must include:

- `supply_score`: 0-100, higher is easier to source and execute
- `supply_notes`: short explanation

## Inputs

Score these factors:

- structure_complexity
- tooling_difficulty
- sku_size_complexity
- qc_difficulty
- aftersales_risk
- logistics_risk
- supplier_findability

## Guidance

- Simple passive parts with common manufacturing processes score higher.
- High SKU complexity, tight fit tolerance, high return risk, or hard-to-find suppliers score lower.
- Large bulky low-ticket products score low even if demand exists.
