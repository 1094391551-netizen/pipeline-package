# Hard Exclusions

Reject immediately:

- phone accessories
- ordinary storage
- ordinary home decor
- high-risk food-contact parts
- flammable parts
- high-temperature/heating parts
- knives/blades
- high-pressure parts
- batteries
- child-safety regulated products
- medical-treatment-claim products
- pure decorative parts
- large high-freight low-ticket products
- obvious brand-infringing copies

If uncertain, downgrade to `REJECT` and record the reason.
