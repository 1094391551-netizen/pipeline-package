# Opportunity Grading

Every opportunity must receive an `A`, `B`, or `C` grade.

## A - Can Deep Dive Now

Use only when:

- Pre-SIF Gate passes.
- Sif demand/root/competition/demand timing is acceptable.
- Amazon ASIN or competitor link is present.
- Offsite evidence exists.
- At least one differentiation path exists.
- Supply and risk scores are acceptable.

## B - Watch / Needs Evidence

Use when:

- Pain point is real but evidence is incomplete.
- Sif data is weak, missing, or inconclusive.
- Amazon front page is mixed or partially homogeneous.
- Supply/risk needs further verification.

## C - Reject

Use when:

- Pre-SIF Gate fails.
- Keyword is generic or repeated.
- Candidate is in rejected blacklist.
- No differentiation path exists.
- Hard exclusion or severe risk is present.

Each grade must include a clear reason.
