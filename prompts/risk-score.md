# Risk Score

Every opportunity must include:

- `risk_score`: 0-100, higher means riskier
- `risk_flags`: list of specific risk labels

## Risk Flags

Evaluate:

- trademark_risk
- patent_risk
- safety_risk
- certification_risk
- platform_policy_risk
- aftersales_liability_risk

## Guidance

- Obvious brand-copy, patented, regulated, safety-critical, high-pressure, battery, heating, child-safety, or medical-treatment products should be `C`.
- Compatibility language is acceptable only when it is clear, accurate, and non-deceptive.
